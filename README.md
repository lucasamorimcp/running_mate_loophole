Replication materials
Earmarked for Her, Received by Him: The Running-Mate Loophole in Gendered Electoral Financing

This folder contains the analysis script, source data, documentary sources, and figures used in the article and its Supplementary Material.

replication.R
R script that reproduces the analyses, first in the order of the article and then in the order of the Supplementary Material.

consulta_cand_YYYY.zip
Candidate registries from Brazil's Superior Electoral Court (TSE). Municipal elections: 2000, 2004, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020, and 2024. General elections: 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, and 2022. The 2026 archive is a September 27, 2026 snapshot used in the introductory discussion.

prestacao_final_2012.zip
Candidate campaign finance records for 2012.

prestacao_contas_final_2016.zip
Candidate campaign finance records for 2016, together with party accounts used to identify donors.

prestacao_de_contas_eleitorais_candidatos_2020.zip
Candidate campaign finance records for 2020.

prestacao_de_contas_eleitorais_candidatos_2024.zip
Candidate campaign finance records for 2024.

fefc_2020_statutory.csv
Statutory allocation of the Special Campaign Financing Fund (FEFC) to each party in 2020, in Brazilian reais.

criterios_fefc_2020_*.pdf and criterios_fefc_2024_*.pdf
Party rules for distributing FEFC resources, used in Supplementary Table S6. The party abbreviation appears in each filename. criterios_fefc_2020_PSD_retificacao.pdf contains a correction to the PSD's rules. These PDFs are documentary sources and are not read by the script.

figure_1.png to figure_5.png
Figures included in the article.

figure_S1.png to figure_S24.png
Figures included in the Supplementary Material.

To reproduce the analyses, keep the files together, leave the ZIP archives compressed, and run replication.R in R or RStudio with this folder as the working directory. Results appear in R. Only the PNG figures are saved, replacing the corresponding images already in this folder.
